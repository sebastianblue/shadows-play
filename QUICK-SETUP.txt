QUICK SETUP
===========

1. Create a blank Google Sheet.
2. Extensions > Apps Script.
3. Paste google-apps-script/Code.gs.
4. Run setupSheet once and approve permissions.
5. Deploy > New deployment > Web app.
6. Execute as: Me. Access: Anyone (if permitted).
7. Copy the /exec URL.
8. Paste it into config.js.
9. Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js and config.js to a static host.
10. Submit a test response and confirm it appears in the Responses tab.
